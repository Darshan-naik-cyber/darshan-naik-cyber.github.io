A reusable outline for write-ups. Copy this file, rename it to `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md`, and replace the content.

## Scope

State what you tested: the model, the application, the tools and data it could reach.

## Method

Describe how you approached it and which techniques you tried.

## Findings

Give each finding a clear title, impact, reproduction steps and a recommended fix.

## Takeaways

End with what defenders and builders should do differently.
