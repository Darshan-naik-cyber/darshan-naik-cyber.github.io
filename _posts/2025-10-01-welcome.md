### Tech Embedded in College

My college operates a centralized server that keeps all the data safe and secure. Plus, there's a firewall in place — but what if you wanted to get around it? No worries, let me walk you through how this firewall actually functions .

My college's firewall manages who can access the internet. Students get login credentials to go online, and because the policy isn't very strict, pretty much anyone can browse freely. Staff members like professors and department heads have their own separate credentials for internet access. However, with one universal credential, anyone in the college can get online. The firewall at my college manages internet access for everyone. Students receive login details to connect to the internet, and thanks to a relaxed policy, access is pretty open for everyone. Professors and department heads use different login credentials for their internet access. That said, with one universal credential available, anyone can gain internet access.

```

```
